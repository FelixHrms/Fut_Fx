Sovereign bond futures from EMIR, decisions
===========================================

Table: lab_prj_emir_ecb.hermesf_fut, built by 02_creating_table.ipynb.
One row per trade state and reporting day, pre-REFIT and REFIT stacked.

Scope
-----
- Dates: pre-REFIT 2018-01-01 to 2024-04-26, REFIT 2025-01-01 to 2026-09-01.
  The REFIT transition window (2024-04-29 to 2024-12-31) is excluded. Positions were
  not carried over properly at the go live and only disappeared as contracts expired.
- Euro area: Eurex sovereign futures identified by ISIN (gov_fut.csv, built from the
  Eurex contract list by 01_gov_futures_list.do). Products FGBS, FGBM, FGBL, FGBX (DE),
  FBTS, FBTM, FBTP (IT), FOAM, FOAT (FR), FBON (ES). Interest rate futures excluded.
- US: CBOT Treasury futures. No contract ISIN exists for third country venues and the
  ISIN and UPI fields are empty or junk, so contracts are identified by venue XCBT,
  contract type FUTR, asset class INTR, CFI FFDPSX and currency USD. product_id is
  synthetic (XCBT_FFDPSX_ plus expiry), expiration_date is the EMIR maturity date.
  The tenor (2y, 5y, 10y, bond) cannot be identified reliably and is not attempted.
- Filters inherited from the colleague's query: direction BYER or SLLR, notional
  strictly positive and at most 1e11. Asset class, contract type, CFI and currency
  filters are not needed for the euro area side, the ISIN list implies them.
- Not used: LEI type filter (all IDs are LEIs), clearing member classification,
  parent entities, lifecycle fields (action type, event type, level, timestamps).

Counterparties
--------------
- Reporter and other counterparty are reallocated to buyer and seller using the
  direction field. Direction is BYER or SLLR in both regimes.
- Sector from xlab_ecb_prj_sftds_cb_common.pth_sector. The three Eurex LEIs are
  overridden to sector CCP. No other CCP list.
- Country from the GLEIF country code fields.

Trade terms
-----------
- notional, notional_currency, price, quantity from the leg1 fields.
  Price currency and price multiplier are dropped, they are null in most rows.
  quantity is field 2.60 (total notional quantity of leg 1), which is the ETD
  quantity field under REFIT. It is null in about 16 percent of rows.
- leg1 fields are used throughout. The best_ fields are only marginally better
  populated and leg2 is mostly empty.

Cleaning rules (applied inside the queries)
-------------------------------------------
1. Expiry cut off. Rows reported more than 10 days after the contract's expiration
   date are dropped. Millions of stale positions were never terminated.
2. Price normalisation. Price is brought to a per 100 quotation by dividing by 10,
   100 or 1,000 when above 250, and only kept if between 50 and 250. Where the own
   price is missing or invalid, a reference price is used, the average normalised
   price of all reporters for the same contract and day.
3. Notional rebuild. Where notional / (quantity x price) > 3000 the notional is
   replaced by quantity x 1000 x price (quantity treated as contracts of 100k face).
   Correct reports sit at 1000 (quantity in contracts) or 1 (quantity in units of
   100 face). Several reporters inflate notional by 100 to 1,000,000.
   Rows without quantity keep the reported notional.
4. Cap. Rows with more than 500,000 implied contracts (notional / (price x 1000))
   are dropped. 28 such rows carried 1.7 tn of notional, none involving the CCP.
   Rows between 100k and 500k contracts are kept, they are consistent with clearing
   members' omnibus positions.
- Raw values are kept as notional_raw and price_raw for traceability.

Known remaining issues
----------------------
- Single large positions appear and vanish (e.g. ES 2023, FR and IT 2025), to be
  handled case by case.
- Level shift at the REFIT restart in January 2025.
- The CCP net position is not exactly flat. Not investigated.
